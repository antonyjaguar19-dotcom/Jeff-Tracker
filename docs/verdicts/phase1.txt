# Phase 1: resolution was the confound

Matching CoTracker3's internal resolution roughly HALVES Jeff-Tracker's
visible error and its re-acquire error, on all four benches.

At 384x512 Jeff-Tracker beats CoTracker3 on visible mean, visible median
and re-acquire EVERYWHERE -- 0.62-0.66 px median against 1.17-1.30.

CoTracker3 keeps two advantages: occluded accuracy, still ~1.5x better,
and a rock-steady tail -- its worst visible error is 5.4-5.8 px on every
bench while ours ranges 6.8 to 91.8.

Attention scope is a NON-FACTOR. Training used 256 tracks per chunk and
inference defaults to 64; matching them moves nothing past the third
decimal. That bounds what the cross-track block is currently doing.

~ 384x512 costs 10.2 GB against 2.8 GB -- CoTracker3's own bracket, so the
~ fair comparison is also the expensive one.
